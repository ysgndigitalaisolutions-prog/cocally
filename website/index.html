FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast does the AI call a new lead?

Within seconds, 24/7, inside legal calling hours. Leads called within 5 minutes are ~21× more likely to qualify — the average business takes over a day.

Is AI outbound calling even legal?

Yes — with consent, disclosure and hygiene, all of which the platform enforces: DNC washing, legal hours, AI self-identification, instant opt-outs, and a compliance log on every call. Built for your own leads and consented lists.

What happens when the AI finds a buyer?

The live call bridges to your best available agent in under a second, briefing on screen. Nobody free? The AI books a time instead.

How is this different from AI SDR tools that overpromised?

A human closes every deal, and everything is verifiable — recorded, transcribed, scored, QA'd, logged. You don't take the AI's word for it. You press play.

Will it sound like a robot?

You pick the voice — up to premium ElevenLabs. It remembers every answer, never re-asks, works objections from your playbook — and tells people it's an AI, which prospects respect.

Does it replace our sales team?

It replaces their dialing, not their closing. Bookings per agent-hour go up — headcount doesn't go down.

How does pricing work?

Platform plan from A$249/mo with free AI minutes included, then a per-minute rate. No per-seat fees, no seat minimums. Enterprise can pay per qualified transfer.
