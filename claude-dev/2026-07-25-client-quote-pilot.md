### 1. What you get

An AI voice agent that dials your lists, screens every call (detects humans vs. voicemail,
navigates IVR/DTMF, filters carrier spam flags), holds a live conversation with the customer, and
**warm-transfers only the connected, interested prospects to your human experts** — so your team
spends its time closing, not dialing. You keep your own telephony/carrier; we charge for the
platform only.

### 3. What each fee covers (no double-charging)

To keep billing unambiguous:

- **The per-minute fee (₹8.50) applies ONLY to live AI-to-customer conversation time** — the meter
  starts when a person answers and the AI begins talking, and stops when the call ends or is
  transferred to your expert.
- **Everything else is covered by the per-dial fee (₹0.80)** — ringing, answering-machine
  detection, voicemail handling, IVR/DTMF navigation, spam checks, and holding the channel during
  and after transfer to your human agent.
- **The human-to-human leg (after transfer) is NOT charged per minute** — that is your agent on
  your carrier.

> Example — one dial that reaches a customer, 90-second AI chat, then transfer:
> ₹0.80 (screening) + 1.5 × ₹8.50 (conversation) = **₹13.55**. A dial that hits voicemail = **₹0.80**.
